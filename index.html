This is an early test version downloaded outside the Play Store, so Android may ask you to confirm the download or installation. Keep Play Protect switched on.

A few things to know before trying it

This is a fresh journal with no one else’s notes or recordings. The introduction includes a clearly labelled fictional example. A moment you write and save is kept in your own journal.

You can try writing, voice notes and the guided example without downloading an AI model. Live AI questions aren’t set up in this download; those need a separate model.

Saved moments stay on your device. Personal Lab recording starts off. Optional speech-to-text asks for your consent and may use your phone’s speech service to process audio.

The app supports ARM64 Android phones running Android 6 or later. If your phone says it isn’t compatible, there’s no need to change any security settings.
