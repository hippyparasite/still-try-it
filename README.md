# Try Still on your Android phone

**[Open the simple download page](https://hippyparasite.github.io/still-try-it/)**

Or **[download INSTALL-STILL-ON-ANDROID.apk](https://hippyparasite.github.io/still-try-it/INSTALL-STILL-ON-ANDROID.apk)** directly.

1. Open the page on your Android phone and tap **Download Still for Android**.
2. Open **INSTALL-STILL-ON-ANDROID.apk** from your browser’s downloads, then tap **Install**. If Android asks, allow this browser to install the app.
3. Tap **Open**, or find **Still — Try Me** on your phone. Follow the introduction and try a made-up moment.

This is an early test version downloaded outside the Play Store. Android may ask you to confirm the download or installation. Keep Play Protect switched on.

Your journal starts empty, with no one else’s notes or recordings. No account is needed. The guided example is fictional; moments you deliberately save are kept in your own journal. Personal Lab recording starts off.

Writing, voice notes and the guided example do not require an AI model. Live AI questions need a separate model and are not set up in this download. Optional speech-to-text asks for consent and may use your phone’s speech service to process audio.

For ARM64 Android phones running Android 6 or later. **Not for iPhone.**
